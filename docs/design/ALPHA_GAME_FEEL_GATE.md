# Alpha Game-Feel Gate

Status: **CANON — certification protocol.** Referenced by
[`DRAGON_VISION_COUNCIL_V1.md`](./DRAGON_VISION_COUNCIL_V1.md) Round 10.D
as the co-gate for `DRAGON_DRIVEN_ALPHA_CERTIFIED`.

## Why this gate exists

A green Playwright journey proves **function**, not feeling. Without a
protocol, "a human played it" degrades into "the owner opened it and
did not complain" — exactly the "technically green, emotionally fake"
failure the Vision Council was convened to prevent.

## Protocol

1. **One human tester** (initially the project owner is fine). The
   tester must not be someone who implemented the systems being judged.
2. **Fresh guest account.** The player-honest path only: no
   `/admin/grant`, no dev unlocks, no fixtures. Dev pacing
   (`DEV_FAST_TIME`) is permitted so the journey fits one session.
3. **No implementation explanations** before or during play. Nobody may
   say what a system is "supposed to feel like."
4. The tester plays the full Alpha Experience Contract journey
   (Council Round 10.D): mystery → Scar → hatchling → growth → Guard
   Harness → Home Guard → Fen Crossing → yield the spawning bank →
   pact → Brinehold → Ford/Blockade stationing.
5. **Immediately after play**, ask the ten questions below, in order,
   verbatim, without leading. Record the answers verbatim in
   `delivery/evidence/` and link that record from the certification
   claim.

## The ten questions

1. What happened at the Scar?
2. Did it feel like surviving a dragon, or like another camp fight?
3. What did you learn about your hatchling?
4. Why did it grow?
5. What changed after you crafted the Guard Harness?
6. Why did the Fen Wyrm accept the pact?
7. What did you give up for it?
8. What changes when the Fen Wyrm leaves Brinehold?
9. Which dragon felt like *your* dragon?
10. What do you want to see next?

## Gates

- **PASS:** the tester explains the world consequences in their own
  words, unprompted — what was surrendered, why the wyrm accepted
  terms, what absence means for the defender and for the attacker.
- **FAIL:** the tester describes the experience mainly as buttons,
  quests, stat buffs, or unlocks.
- Questions **2, 6, 7, and 8** are the **identity beats** (truthful
  encounter, pact reason, real sacrifice, absence intelligence). A
  button-shaped answer to any of these blocks certification even if the
  overall session reads positively.
- A failed gate may be re-run after fixes; the re-run replaces the
  record. Repairs must not consist of copy that *explains* a fantasy
  the play itself was supposed to deliver.

## What this gate does not test

Balance, multi-day pacing, comprehension of deferred systems, and
retention. Those belong to **First Watch** (Council Round 10.I,
campaign 3). This gate certifies that the Alpha's authored fantasy is
actually experienced — not that the game is done.
