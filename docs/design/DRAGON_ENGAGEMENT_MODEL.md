# Dragon Engagement Model

Status: **CANON — FROZEN** retention principles.
**CANON — PROVISIONAL** named-wild examples.

The authored dragon / settlement spine is not the entire long-term
game. If the only reason to log in is "unlock the next city," the
design has failed the finite-campaign test.

Rejected default live-ops: new colored dragon every season, gacha
banners, chest tracks as the retention engine.

---

## Daily (15–40 minutes)

A meaningful check-in, not a chore wheel.

Possible:

- roost state (temperament, injury treatment choice, not hunger-death)
- rumors and incomplete sightings
- wound treatment step
- preparation (fitting Escort vs Yard, provisioning a later march)
- one short observation that can move a knowledge state
- alliance mail about a dragon absence

Avoid:

- punitive daily feeding
- mandatory login streaks as the bond
- five collect-a-thons

Alpha: roost state + injury/treatment if wounded + one rumor is
enough.

---

## Weekly

- one major dragon deployment (signature escort, or Mirecrown
  blockade request)
- a hunt or wild observation in Cinderreach woods
- a doctrine decision (who guards the roost if the hatchling is Away)
- a bestiary discovery from a fight that was not farmed
- an alliance operation

Weekly should create Chronicle lines, not energy-bar clears.

---

## Multi-week / seasonal

- migrations (Leanwing thermal roads, wild Valley Drake movements)
- named wild individuals appearing, moving, dying, returning
- ecology changes (flood year helps Mirecrown, drought hurts the pact)
- dragon sightings that are not the player's
- regional crises
- alliance wars that care where major dragons are
- weather that grounds Leanwings or cracks Fen silt-pack

These are Layer C's job. They keep the map alive after Galeari is
founded.

---

## Named wild individuals

Rare, authored or lightly generated, **not collectibles**.

Examples (provisional):

- an old scarred predator nesting near Cinderreach
- a pale migrant crossing multiple realms (may be *a* Leanwing that
  is not Pale Passage)
- a wounded dragon around an alliance objective
- a recurring wild rival of the signature dragon
- an unknown species observation that stays UNKNOWN for a long time

They may move, survive, be killed, disappear, return, generate
research, create political conflict.

Killing one is a political and ecological fact. It is not a loot roll
and does not add a Layer B roster slot by default.

This is the critical answer to "campaign completes, then quits."

---

## Why return after authored holdings are done

| Horizon | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Tomorrow | roost, wound, rumor, one observation |
| Next week | a deployment, a hunt, a Chronicle event |
| Next month | a migration, a named wild, an alliance war that cares about absence |
| After three months | a new named wild, a seasonal ecology, the signature dragon's Veteran stories, a realm crisis that is not "Dragon 6" |
| After the spine | Layer C + PvP + research arms race + Chronicle prestige |

If a season ships only a recolored dragon, reject the season.

---

## Low-population and solo

The design must work when the realm is quiet.

| Situation | What still works |
| --- | --- |
| Low-population realm | wild ecology, named individuals, PvE hunts, hatchling growth, Mirecrown river as PvE geography, research from observation |
| No alliance | personal Chronicle, roost, domain pacts, scoutable AI/wild threats; alliance-scale Karth waits |
| Quiet PvP | absence windows still matter against camps and NPC lords if those exist; against players, even one neighbor makes Ironspine's absence juicy |

Do not require a full alliance to raise the hatchling or pact
Mirecrown.

---

## PvP conflict created by this model

- raid windows from scoutable absence
- river blockade as politics
- intercept of moving armies (later)
- knowledge arms race (Predictive Volley vs Leanwing users)
- slayer vs pact reputations
- Titan wake as a realm event other alliances must answer
- named wilds on contested objectives

PvP remains army-fundamental. Dragons change the *map*, not the
requirement to bring troops.
