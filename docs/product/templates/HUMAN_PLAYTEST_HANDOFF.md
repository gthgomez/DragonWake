# Human Playtest Handoff

Status: **CURRENT AUTHORITY** — lightweight one-page form for a
`HUMAN_PLAYER` session (see evaluator classes in
[`../FTUE_PLAYTEST_PROTOCOL.md`](../FTUE_PLAYTEST_PROTOCOL.md)). Designed
to be used repeatedly without coaching the participant toward known
issues.

## Instructions for the organizer

1. Hand the participant the **Participant card** below, the game URL, and
   nothing else. No hints, no feature names, no "pay attention to…".
2. Observe quietly; note times and quotes. Do not explain mid-session.
3. Fill the observer section immediately afterwards; ask the participant
   to fill theirs before any discussion.
4. Attach a run manifest
   ([`PLAYTEST_RUN_MANIFEST.md`](PLAYTEST_RUN_MANIFEST.md)) with
   `EVALUATOR_CLASS: HUMAN_PLAYER`.

---

## Participant card (give this to the player)

> Play this game for about 30–60 minutes. It is okay to feel lost — just
> keep going as best you can and say what you're thinking. We are not
> testing you; we are testing the game. Afterwards we'll ask you a few
> short questions.

### Post-session questions (ask in order, record verbatim)

1. In one sentence: what is this game?
2. What did you do first, and why?
3. What are you trying to accomplish next?
4. What did the dragon content feel like — was there a dragon moment?
5. Where were you confused or blocked?
6. What felt like a real game? What felt like a prototype?
7. Would you come back tomorrow? Why / why not?
